Local Service

Local flat pack assembly, Mornington & nearby

Mornington and the northern Peninsula suburbs have family homes, holiday houses and downsizers' units. People are often furnishing a place before summer or setting up a second home, which means a lot of boxes and not much time.

We assemble indoor furniture, wardrobes, outdoor settings and flat pack sheds, and take the packaging away on request. If you're setting up a holiday house, tell us the dates and we'll work around them.

Planning a shed? Check with the Mornington Peninsula Shire before you order, as larger sheds can need approval. Sheds also need a level, prepared base before we arrive.
